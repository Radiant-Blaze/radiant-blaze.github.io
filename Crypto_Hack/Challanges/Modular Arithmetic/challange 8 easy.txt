Goal:
Find √x mod p

Case 1:
p ≡ 3 (mod 4)

Use:

root = pow(x, (p+1)//4, p)

----------------------------

Case 2:
p ≡ 1 (mod 4)

Use:

Tonelli-Shanks Algorithm

----------------------------

Tonelli-Shanks

Input:
x, p

Output:
√x mod p

----------------------------

Works only for PRIME p.

Does NOT work for composite numbers.

----------------------------

Used in:
✔ Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC)
✔ Finding coordinates on elliptic curves
