# Contributing to Radiant Blaze

Thank you for your interest in Radiant Blaze!

## Project Maintenance

Radiant Blaze is a personal project maintained by its original author.

Development, maintenance, design decisions, and project direction are managed by the maintainer, with assistance from AI tools.

## AI-Assisted Development

AI tools may be used to assist with:

- Writing and reviewing code.
- Debugging and troubleshooting.
- Research and documentation.
- Design suggestions and improvements.
- Testing and development workflows.

All AI-assisted changes are reviewed and managed by the project maintainer.

AI tools are not considered independent authors or human contributors.

## Contributions

This repository is primarily maintained as a personal project and does not actively seek external contributors.

Issues may be used for reporting bugs, sharing suggestions, or discussing improvements. External pull requests are not actively solicited and may be declined.
